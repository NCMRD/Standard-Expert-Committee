# Materials R&D Data Standard Expert Committee

### Introduction
**Materials R&D Data Standard Expert Committee** builds standard schema and vocabularies of materials R&D data. The committee was launched in March 2021, as an affiliated organization of the **National Center for Materials Research Data (NCMRD)**, designated by the Ministry of Science and ICT in 2020. For more information on the data standardization efforts of NCMRD, please refer to the NCMRD website (ncmrd.re.kr).

### Publicaton History
Following versions of the dictionary were published upto now : 
 1) (NCMRD SD21-01) Standard Vocabulary for Materials Research Data (Dec. 2021, 1st Ed.)
 2) (NCMRD SD22-01) Standard Vocabulary for Materials Research Data (Jul. 2022, 2nd Ed.)
 3) (NCMRD SD22-02) Standard Vocabulary for Materials Research Data (Feb. 2023, 3rd Ed.)
 4) (NCMRD SD23-01) Standard Vocabulary for Materials Research Data (Sep. 2023, 4th Ed.)
 5) (NCMRD SD24-01) Standard Vocabulary for Materials Research Data (Mar. 2024, 5th Ed.)
 6) (NCMRD SD24-02) Standard Vocabulary for Materials Research Data (Oct. 2024, 6th Ed.)
 7) (NCMRD SD25-01) Standard Vocabulary for Materials Research Data (Apr. 2025, 7th Ed.)

**Materials Data Standard Dictionary** can be downloaded from the NCMRD website (ncmrd.re.kr). The json schema files of the dictionary can be downloaded from the sub-braches of the Main branch.

### Files in Main Branch
Documents related with the standardization of materials research data are uploaded on the main branch : 
 1) Standardization Guidelines : Specific guides and regulations for building standard schema and vocabularies
 2) Presentation Materials : Viewgraphs of the presentation materials on materials research data standardization 
 3) Papers : Published papers on materials research data standardization
